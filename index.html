Ludo

Unfinished game

Play against the computer, or pass the device between friends.

How to play
  • Roll a 6 to move a token out of your yard.
  • Move all 4 tokens around the board and into your home column to win.
  • Landing on an opponent sends them back to their yard, except on ★ and start squares, which are safe.
  • You get another roll when you roll a 6, capture a token, or bring a token home. Rolling three 6s in a row ends your turn.
  • You need the exact number to reach home.
  • Online: create a room and send the link or code to friends. Each person controls their own color.
  • Keyboard: Space rolls the dice, 1–4 pick a token.
